Sound Garden is a playful place to make music. Pick sounds, arrange them in your garden, and listen as your little ideas grow into a song. No music lessons or studio experience needed.

## Make music your way

- **Plant sound seeds:** Mix beats, bass, dreamy chords, and bright melodies. Move them around to shape your song.
- **Find your sound:** Try Pop, Hip-hop, Rock, or 8-bit sounds, then change any instrument on its own.
- **Build a chord journey:** Choose a musical mood or pick a chord path; pitched sounds follow the harmony as your song changes.
- **Play along:** Use your computer keyboard, the on-screen keys, or a connected MIDI keyboard.
- **Jam together:** Share a room link so friends can join your song from their own browsers.
- **Make it yours:** Change the colors, adjust the sounds, and save your finished song as audio.

## Come on in

Open **[Sound Garden](https://jdamboeck.github.io/noobie-daw/)** in a modern browser. Press play, try a sound seed, and see what grows. Sound Garden works best with a recent version of Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.

## For curious growers

To run Sound Garden on your own computer, install Node.js and npm, then run:

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

To make a production build, run `npm run build`. Pushing changes to `main` automatically builds and publishes the site with [GitHub Pages](.github/workflows/deploy-pages.yml).
